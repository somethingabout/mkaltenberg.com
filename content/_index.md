I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Pace University in New York, NY. My research focuses on innovation and labor economics. My research questions are largely driven by curiosity about how innovation reshapes work and the structure of the economy, and how policy impacts the lives of inventors and women.

I primarily teach quantitative methods in economics to undergraduate and graduate students. In research collaborations, I tend to collect and develop novel metrics for difficult-to-measure variables, and to develop methodology for research questions centered on causal inference econometrics and network statistics.
