EconHack is an annual policy hackathon I organize at Pace University, co-hosted with the Economics Society and Women in Economics. Student teams are given a live policy problem, a set of data sources, and roughly a day to design an evidence-based intervention, which they present to a panel of faculty judges.

Each year's problem, data sources, and winning presentations are archived below.
